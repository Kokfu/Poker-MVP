# Phase 5F — Frozen evidence-aware Profile V2 protocol

## Forecasts

A is the unchanged Phase 5E pooled-profile comparator `phase_5e.incumbent`, with its existing add-one smoothing and legal aggression split. This is the research reply baseline used to measure Phase 5E's EV loss. Production Coach uses CFR, and the separate Phase 5D sensitivity observer uses its existing hand- and price-tilted law. B is unchanged Phase 5E V2, including its two-count Dirichlet prior from the other cells on the same street and menu, add-one pooling and uniform empty-pool behavior. C invokes B for supported wagers and returns A exactly for sparse or unsupported queries. D is the diagnostic synthetic response law, available only to generate simulated observations and score forecasts. All models share the public state and exact legal response classes. The evaluator divides class mass evenly among concrete legal targets of the same class.

Use the full V2 situation key and exact reduced ratio of collectible wager to matched pot. Situation includes street, responder position, previous applied action, aggression depth, V2 price/depth/SPR buckets, closure and normalized legal response mask. The exact ratio guards bucket aliases at unseen sizes. Stack and SPR use the existing bucket boundaries. Known legal opportunities and valid observed replies are counted separately from hands, with certified completed histories and stable source IDs. An all-in call is a call, and chips in excess of a short stack never count as collectible. Free nodes and nonpositive or absent matched pots provide no live-wager support. The candidate does not infer any feature from unseen holdings or reference laws.

Supported means at least 20 valid responses at the matching evidence key. Sparse means 1–19. Unsupported means zero, including missing V2 and legal exposures with no valid response. Set 20 before holdout by rounding 19 up to a multiple of five. At 19 responses the inherited two-count prior first represents less than 10% of the effective sample, and at 20 the worst-case binomial standard error is about 11 percentage points. This counts usable samples and is neither a calibrated confidence measure nor a correction for missing-not-at-random responses. Retain B's original bucket-level estimate after selection. Distinct supported ratios inside one bucket can still share a conditional estimate. No blending, retuning, profile overwrite, strategy hook, schema change or rollout.

## Action values and gates

Use a uniform incoming range over the 990 blocker-compatible holdings, Treys outcomes, the same pot, stacks, menu, independent cash ledger, and fixed Hero continuation (fold to later aggression, otherwise check). The same deterministic label-tied greedy root choice applies to all arms. Each checked-to root wager's only reached future reply is its opponent node under this Hero tail, so its action value must exactly reproduce A under C fallback. Break out action errors by exact supported, sparse and unsupported coverage, no-future-reply controls and wager price group.

The primary endpoint is C minus A in mean per-decision MAE across all legal concrete actions, including closing controls. Require at least 0.10 bb improvement with the upper 95% paired interval strictly below zero. Use 2,000 paired percentile bootstrap draws with seed 894100, resampling the two independent training clusters within each family/exposure stratum. Report B minus A and C minus A for decision MAE, regret, realized multiclass Brier and log loss. Clip probabilities at 1e-12 solely to score log loss. Report expected proper scores, individual fold/call/raise/shove MAE, modal closing reply accuracy, and realized versus predicted response frequency by price and support. Report paired differences and inspect the most harmful recommendations individually.

Require at least 32 action instances and 32 reply scores per supported, sparse and unsupported slice. C must match A exactly on every fallback probability and directly affected wager EV. Mean C-minus-A oracle recommendation regret may increase at most 0.05 bb, and no switch may add 8 bb or more. Report both absolute worst regret and incremental switch harm. A material action pair has an oracle gap above .01 bb. Correct ranking requires the predicted gap to clear .01 bb in the same direction. Count aggressive false positives where estimated EV clears the passive maximum by .01 bb and true EV falls below it by .01 bb. A profitable fold-dependent bluff beats the passive maximum by .01 bb and trails it by .01 bb when all folds become continuations. A closing call is profitable above zero bb. Detect unprofitable shoves by predicted deficit greater than .01 bb to the best passive action when the oracle agrees.

## Reliability and reproducibility

Use one numerical thread per process. Freeze SHA-256 for the protocol and core study sources before running the fresh panels. Use hash seeds 13 and 31 with reverse cluster order for 31 and exact byte equality of canonical results. Retain scheduled rows and timing failures. Run memory instrumentation separately so its allocation overhead stays out of the holdout timing. Maximum offline evaluation time per deal is 15 s and per-worker memory is 768 MiB.

Run the Phase 5E real Coach workload once alone and then in four simultaneous fresh processes, each with its own store and trace directory and a 360 s batch watchdog. Each worker probes archived rows 78 and 31 and quarter-pot and pot controls with three enabled and one disabled call per case. Require complete schedule, exactly 12 completed normalized enabled traces per worker, zero errors/missing traces/timeouts or unexpected production fallbacks, unchanged public advice between paired settings, all Coach calls below 15 s, and observer processing below 150 ms excluding trace write. Record import, one-hand replay, capture, CFR, shadow evaluation, trace writing, cold-board and warm-board latency and peak resident bytes. A four-worker pass applies only to the measured workload. Inspect pre-existing forced-error, timeout and trace-write isolation regressions. The native deadline is cooperative and bounded by the earlier of the 150 ms compute budget and the 15 s end-to-end reference. A scheduler stall or blocking disk operation can exceed elapsed deadlines. Keep the real call path and budgets unchanged.

## Decision

A requires all numerical, downside, coverage, runtime and reproducibility gates and complete regression evidence. Recommend Phase 5G research integration and gameplay validation. B applies when forecast or EV gains are demonstrated but any required gate or evidence is missing, with one targeted repair. C applies when the evidence fails to show a credible forecast-to-value benefit and warrants a different observation/model approach. All outcomes preserve existing strategy defaults and learned profiles.
