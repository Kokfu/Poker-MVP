# Phase 4Z frozen river action-EV protocol

Freeze date 19 November 2026. The goal is to determine whether the current Coach can distinguish genuinely profitable individual actions from actions that look profitable under its own range and reply forecast. The production Coach and Match retain their current strategy and defaults. Historical replays and deterministic settlement controls are diagnostic. There is no strategy candidate or promotion test.

## Independent population

- Six new matched boards and Hero holdings from deal seed 450202, crossed with training streams 341–343 and the four fixed tight/loose × passive/aggressive reference styles, for 72 decisions. All styles travel together within each board-stream cell. Every board has one checked-to, one live half-pot wager and one closing wager geometry. Use the Phase 4P/4Q preflop paths and flop/turn lines. Checked-to rows put Hero in position and replace the observed river wager with a check. Live rows with insufficient chips behind use 30 bb. Other starting stacks use the inherited 9/15/30 bb schedule. Prefix 0/3/30/100 rotates by board and stream index. Training uses preceding synthetic public evidence only.
- Build the true range by enumerating all 990 compatible holdings with Treys rank and weights given by the product of Phase 4P preflop and flop/turn likelihoods and Phase 4Q river opening or check likelihood. Hero's scripted past action contributes no private-card likelihood. The four response policies use the Phase 4K call rates conditional on strength and collectible price. Passive opponents fold or call. Aggressive opponents also choose a legal effective shove at rates 2%, 10% and 35% by tier, halved above pot, and divide the remaining mass by the same call law. This is a Phase 4M-style reference with aggression restricted to the two aggressive styles. Public profile streams are generated by the existing Phase 4K synthetic opponent and its scripted training process. They are proxy public evidence under a different action mix, so sample size alone cannot identify the reference law.
- Evaluate the unmodified legacy Coach path and existing disabled-by-default persistent current-root path. Use the existing replay chooser seed 917, action legalization and full-precision mixes. Replay the incumbent seeded action as the common comparator within each decision. Evaluate an expanded checked-to search menu with quarter, half, three-quarter, full and one-and-a-half pot plus effective all-in as a sizing diagnostic. Keep the later menu unchanged. This diagnostic is outside the primary population summaries and has no deployment or promotion significance.
- Wager buckets use collectible incremental price divided by the matched pot before the wager. Small ≤0.33, medium >0.33 and <0.75, large ≥0.75 and <1, pot =1, and overbet >1. Closing effective shoves take precedence. Price closing covers, nonclosing calls, checks and folds separately.

## Independent accounting and fixed interventions

- Use net bb from the current decision, starting stack change after refund and award. Use a cash ledger independent of CFR payoff traversal, Treys versus solver showdown matrix agreement, independent exact fold/call checks on every native action, and deterministic engine controls for ties, raises, short covers and refunds. Enumerate every legal integer root wager with the fixed fold/call reference and also score the engine's distinct all-in option, including short covers. Where Villain may raise, branch-exact comparisons use the fixed finite menus and the existing Hero continuation from that solve. There is no oracle free sizing after the initial wager. An all-legal-target search uses the fold/call reference to isolate root sizing costs. Regret for a recommendation uses the best action in its fixed menu under the same continuation.
- For each fixed menu, hold the Hero continuation, baseline action, and action ranking rule fixed. A uses the full estimated range and estimated replies, B swaps in the reference range, C swaps in reference replies and D uses both. Use the inherited nearest-rank reply extension for omitted columns in A and B. Log actual projected action EV and full-precision solver EV separately. The projected-to-full contrast and a 384-versus-896 working-set rerun measure different effects. The rerun also changes the estimated replies through CFR. Deployable advice receives no oracle inputs.
- For each fold/call branch compute the marginal fold rate required to equal the same incumbent action, conditional on the model's or reference's calling composition. Use the collectible contribution after refunds, all showdown results including ties, and net reward if Villain folds. Record forecast, reference fold rate, both thresholds and both margins. A threshold outside [0,1] indicates no interior break-even. Branches with raises get fold/call/raise rates without a two-outcome threshold.

## Outcomes and uncertainty

- Primary action outcomes are signed and absolute error, rank concordance among pairs separated by >0.01 bb, and positive/negative sign errors at a ±0.01 bb threshold. Exclude the zero self-contrast. Report mean predicted and exact advantage by size, style, profile prefix, public line, depth, SPR and decision geometry. Report major errors by individual action and full public case key. Separately report mixed EV, modal EV, and actual seeded recommendation EV, each with regret. Break modal ties lexically. Count any recommendation more than 0.01 bb below its best menu action and severe regrets >0.50 bb. Flag profitable covers folded and losing covers taken. The realized outcome of an individual played hand does not enter these conditional EV estimates.
- For decomposition report the error after each of A/B/C/D, ranking reversals, interaction D−C−B+A, working-set sensitivity, and menu gap under the common fold/call law. Keep identities for worst regret and paired losses. Account for actual draw conversion separately. A material structural bug requires evidence beyond regular numerical precision.
- The exploratory advantage envelope uses only public-input alternatives: native point estimate, projected/full/uniform/pre-river-observation ranges where available, the frozen Phase 4U independent reply scenario family, and the 384-column sensitivity for the persistent replay. Evaluate empirical coverage and mean width for non-self contrasts. These are scenario ranges with no nominal confidence level. Report coverage by prefix, history, price and all-in status. Shared structural assumptions can miss the true EV even with mature profiles. No hidden range or reply data enter the envelope.
- Use a crossed 10,000-resample percentile bootstrap with seed 45003 for decision-weighted primary MAE, mean regrets and envelope coverage. Resample boards and training streams independently and retain all four styles per cell. With six boards and three training streams, inference is descriptive conditional on this population. Never interpret this bootstrap as a per-action uncertainty interval, or as evidence of live-opponent calibration.

## Execution and decision

- Freeze file hashes and the full schedule before the fresh run. Verify the manifest on each run. Execute with hash seeds 13 and 31, one BLAS thread, warmed board caches, and reversed alternating A/B order across runs. Per-arm advisor time must be ≤15 seconds and the persistent path must complete exactly one root solve with at most 896 columns per seat (384 on its sensitivity arm). Legacy retains its existing working-set rules. The offline runner separately measures analysis time and has a 1,200-second process deadline per run. Record every attempted row and require zero unexpected fallbacks. Require deterministic rows and summaries across seeds, excluding time. Report partial or failed runs explicitly. No tuning on the holdout.
- Reproduce the historical −8.204 bb displayed loss, −9.639 bb seeded shove, −17.690 bb bluff, −2.347 bb harmful accepted mixed change and profitable closing calls folded. Save public line, probabilities, estimated and reference range and reply details, action values, mixes and seeded and modal choices. Preserve the original payoff law for the historical reproduction and label the fresh-study reference overlay separately.
- Choose A only if the action evidence supports reliable individual selection, including the severe downside and sign-error results. Choose B if a concrete repair explains the main reliability failure. Choose C if range and response errors or their interaction continue to produce important false advantages. Prior promotion gates remain in force. Phase 5 design starts from the measured source of failure, with offline engineering cost and decision-time budget spelled out.
