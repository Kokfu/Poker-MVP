Freeze the source hashes and ordered schedule before opening any fresh reference outcomes. The endpoint is the actual sampled Coach action using the native menu and legacy range path. The recommendation seed is 917, consistent with the archived probes. The incumbent Coach runs with the river shadow flag enabled for observation. The flag defaults to off. Research changes are limited to private local observation.

## Frozen diagnostic

- Use the Phase 5B full compatible posterior, bounded payoff traversal, and five existing range and reply stresses. The uniform range mixture is 15%. Reply stresses use paired lower/lower and upper/upper endpoints of the Phase 4Y pooled river counts forecast. Phase 5C adds two closing-wager stresses where the root is fold/call and future reply is absent. Double or halve Hero-win relative to Hero-loss holding odds, applying square-root weights to wins and reciprocal weights to losses with ties weighted at one. Zero-mass holdings stay at zero in those tilts. The uniform stress can restore missing support.
- The selected action margin is its net bb EV minus the best other action EV in each model. Flag if any scenario places the selection at least 2 bb behind an alternative and worsens its margin by at least 1 bb relative to nominal. Report the triggering scenario names. The two tolerances are materiality and movement criteria chosen before holdout, with 2 bb inherited from Phase 5B. No holdout or historical fit is used. Store nominal margin, scenario margins, fraction of stressed models changing the greedy action, effective range size, omitted mass, river reply opportunities and opponent-profile hands. Scenario fractions are deterministic shares of a finite stress set.
- For a wager with positive fold opportunity and a meaningful crossing, report estimated immediate fold probability, the fold frequency required to beat the best passive alternative given the estimated aggregate nonfold EV, their difference, and the collectible cost. This frequency is descriptive and held at fixed nonfold composition. It does not determine the flag. Closing calls use the conditional showdown mixture and exact pot settlement. Weak evidence and effective support are descriptive quantities.
- Compare greedy CFR/shadow disagreement, the Phase 5B sensitivity formula, Phase 5C, a nominal shadow gap of at least 2 bb and a range-support rule below 50. Score all against seeded reference regret. Retain the original Phase 5B greedy-shadow endpoint per row for attribution.

## Independent fresh panel and references

There is one decision per combination of four reference styles, six wager classes and four profile lengths. Seed 653203 shuffles factor assignment to deals. Stream labels start at 8200. Require recall at least 70%, precision at least 55%, severe recall at least 75%, false-positive rate at most 25% and a Wilson lower bound of at least 50% on severe coverage. Evaluate full diagnostic coverage separately. At least 95% of scheduled traces must complete, zero unexpected production fallbacks or observer errors are permitted, and no recommendation or probability may change in the invariance comparisons.
- B requires a pre-specified class (closing or nonclosing) to reach the recall, precision, severe coverage and false-positive point targets with at least 12 high-risk and eight severe examples in the class. Correctness, reproducibility, invariance and resource gates still apply. C otherwise. Insufficient denominators are inconclusive for the associated claims. No verdict authorizes a strategy gate.
