## Decision

A requires every numerical, downside, coverage, runtime, reproducibility and verification gate. Recommend a separate research integration and paired gameplay-validation phase. B applies when conditional forecasts or action values improve but an acceptance gate remains unresolved or fails; identify the specific blocker. C applies when additional data give no credible decision-quality improvement, warranting reassessment of opponent inference. Report the full backend suite, retained Phase 5F IDs, production build, dependency health and offline Coach/Match/Replay invariance. Keep production defaults and prior history fixtures unchanged in every outcome.
