# Phase 4L experiment protocol

Use the same four Phase 4K synthetic opponents and train the same way at 15 bb. Development reuses the four historical spots and stream 0 at 30 hands, including the largest Phase 4K loss. The primary holdout uses the four new scripted boards frozen in the runner, new training streams 9 and 10, and the same 30-hand profile prefix. Cross the four opponents with all four boards and both streams. Report the 32-row 15 bb holdout separately from the 32-row 9 bb and 32-row 30 bb sensitivity panels. Use the same 15 bb training profiles for the depth sensitivity. All rows use Hero on the button, Villain calling Hero's open, two check-through streets, and a Villain river check or 500-chip opening bet. The same reference posterior and response govern scoring within a row. Keep the preflop charts, public replay, river tree and native production solve budgets unchanged.

The factorial baseline uses the final conditional opponent reach and per-combo continuation from adaptive Coach. Treat the unchanged live recommendation and live mix as controls. To hold the response input fixed when switching ranges, freeze the live continuation matrices. Where live pruning omitted a compatible holding, use the mean live response of the nearest retained Treys rank or tied ranks, with equal weight per donor combo. No estimated posterior mass is moved. Report omitted true mass. For the true response, apply Phase 4K's exact price and holding-conditioned fold and call probabilities, with zero river raises. The true posterior weights all 990 legal villain holdings by the known path likelihood. Conditional Villain raises in the estimated-model arms use the fixed live Hero continuation. The four root mixes all use the same 80-step discounted regret-matching update against fixed action EVs. Its purpose is to vary either model input while measuring root selection with a common rule. Live minus A measures the effect of switching from live joint solving to a fixed-continuation root conversion, with A audited against the live read-only action values. Both substitutions are research-only. Score all mixes using the independent Phase 4J terminal evaluator. Search the reference's affine price regions for the best legal integer target. Use 1e-6 bb as the harm threshold. Treat rows as a finite dependent panel.

A root-EV argmax is eligible as a research candidate only if development shows a positive mean true-EV delta over live, a mean gap of at least 0.10 bb under the live action values, and positive true-EV effects in at least three opponent types. Greedy ranking is a diagnostic on both sets regardless of eligibility. If eligible, require holdout improvement of at least 0.10 bb per decision at 15 bb, nonnegative mean deltas in each opponent and depth, and no per-row loss above 0.25 bb. Compare its remaining regret to D. Require legal, normalized policies and zero unexpected fallbacks. Measure the read-only EV pass if the candidate advances because a native gameplay solve can skip it. No production changes or gameplay promotion are part of this experiment.

Cap development and holdout processes externally at 240 and 900 seconds, respectively. Cap focused, research and full backend verification at 240, 900 and 2,400 seconds. Use one BLAS thread and offline inputs. Compare the development replay under a second Python hash seed, excluding performance fields. Preserve prior artifacts.
