# Phase 5E — Frozen opponent-profile evaluation

v2 is an offline, single-writer read model with no API, database migration or strategy hook. The compatible history schema remains 1.0. An absent v2 profile is absent evidence. A caller provides a namespaced opponent identity, immutable source hand ID and the identity's seat per hand. The same ID must survive export or replay. An equal retry is idempotent. Conflicting evidence under that ID fails. Unkeyed histories cannot be safely deduplicated. Validate settlement, schema, event order, state continuity and chips first. Reject corrupt or incomplete hands atomically. A missing legal menu or untrustworthy preceding fallback makes a decision ineligible. Known legal opportunities with missing or fallback responses count as exposures and missing, never as a particular policy action.

Track exact wager evidence transiently per event. Use prior action, street, position, aggression depth capped at three, collectible price relative to matched pot, effective depth in bb, SPR, closing flag and normalized legal action set. Price edges are 0, .375, .75 and 1.125, depth edges 10, 30 and 100 bb, SPR edges 1, 3 and 8. Edges are inclusive on the lower bucket. A forced blind difference is not a voluntary opponent offer. Remove overhang from both price and matched pot. Effective depth is the smaller of the responder stack and the opponent stack plus outstanding price. A live opposing wager is closing when its maker is out of chips or the responder can only call all-in. Normalize all-in calls to call, classify increasing all-ins as shove, and exclude nominal uncollectible aggression against an exhausted maker. Check, bet and raise retain their own classes. Report counts of known legal exposures, usable responses and unknown eligibility. Rates use observed legal responses and missing counts are reported; complete-case rates can be biased by fallback. For a missing-response sensitivity bound use successes/exposures through (successes + missing)/exposures.
