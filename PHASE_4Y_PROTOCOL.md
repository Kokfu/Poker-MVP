## Endpoints and gates

Primary mixed endpoint is paired reference mixed-policy EV in bb per decision. Co-primary individual endpoint is paired reference EV for the seeded concrete action. Require at least +0.05 bb mean and a strictly positive crossed 95% lower bound for each endpoint for a strategy promotion. Modal metrics are descriptive. Independently for each endpoint require worst paired downside no worse than −0.50 bb and no more than 10% of rows losing over 0.01 bb. Using the same downside magnitude for the individual gate applies the existing tolerable risk per decision to the action a user actually receives. It does not average away a severe tail draw. Report harmful probability mass as the mix weight of actions that would cost over 0.50 bb relative to A's displayed action and report maximum supported action regret. An arm must preserve each positive incumbent closing-call probability within 1e-12 and introduce zero new seeded folds on profitable closing calls. Require per-style mixed mean at least −0.03 bb, changed policy coverage, all legal finite normalized policies, reference settlement agreement to 1e-10 bb, complete pairs and zero unexpected fallbacks.

Use the inherited crossed 95% percentile bootstrap with 10,000 resamples and seed 44003. Resample board indices and training streams independently, keeping all styles together. Two training streams bound interpretation of the intervals. Report each opponent's mean, absolute regrets, severe counts, harmful action probabilities, profitable closing-call regressions and worst paired rows. Any failure or partial run is recorded by scheduled key. An incomplete run supplies no promotion estimate.

Use hash seeds 13 and 31, one BLAS thread, warmed matched board caches, alternating replay order reversed on the second run, and an independent 1,200-second process deadline per run. Log every attempted row incrementally. Compare deterministic rows and statistics across seeds, excluding timing and peak RSS. End-to-end arm advice must take at most 15 seconds per decision and a proposed arm must have median at most 2× A and whole-process peak RSS at most 768 MiB. B is capped at 896 CFR columns per seat and exactly one current-root decision solve. A retains the legacy working set. C pays for all replays it requires. Grid, profile generation, and scoring are outside advisor timing. Verify the manifest before every holdout run. A positive mean alone does not authorize promotion. A future qualifying candidate would still require separate gameplay validation.
