# Phase 5A river action evaluator protocol

Frozen 19 November 2026 before the fresh holdout. The research question is whether CFR's action-value calculation, its input models, its action menu, or the final selection rule drives bad river decisions. This phase has no strategy promotion gate. Coach and Match continue to use their current routes and flags.

## Population and contract

- Use deal seed `550202`, six boards, training streams `541–543`, and all four tight/loose × passive/aggressive styles for 72 fresh decisions. Rotate checked-to, live half-pot and closing-shove positions across board and stream. Rotate profile prefixes `0/3/30/100`. Use the existing Phase 4P/4Q public betting lines and 9/15/30 bb stack depths, with a 30 bb substitution where a live bet needs chips behind. Development uses seed `550201` and streams `531–533`. Explicit settlement and pure-bluff controls are outside both panels.
- The contract is the replayed river decision with Hero cards, five board cards, public path, position, pot, current-street commitments, stacks, legal action and target bounds, and the configured finite menus. A combo law is a normalized posterior over physically compatible Villain holdings. Past actions enter via the replay likelihoods. Future replies are normalized per holding and per reachable response node. Hero continuation may use the public branch but never knows the actual Villain holding. Check results and action choices retain the same concrete target-total convention and deterministic chooser seed `917`.
- Value is the change in Hero's remaining cash from the decision before the evaluated action, after refunding uncalled chips and awarding the contested pot. Divide chips by the hand's big blind for net bb. A legal fold starts at zero in these positions. Tied awards follow the engine's odd-chip seat rule. Prior contributions are sunk. A wager that covers the other stack is priced at collectible chips. Record the incumbent seeded concrete action as the reference for both predicted and true advantage.
- Reference weights use all 990 compatible holdings, Treys rank, preflop and flop/turn likelihoods from Phase 4P, and the Phase 4Q river check or opening-wager likelihood through Phase 4Z. The reference reply law uses Phase 4K call probabilities by hand strength and price. Passive styles use fold/call. Aggressive styles also assign 2%, 10% or 35% to legal shoves by strength, halved above pot. All branches and payoffs are determined by explicit inputs. The same frozen law is used for every comparator where supported. Profile evidence comes from the existing synthetic training process and estimates only public evidence. Reverse tightness and aggression for a mismatched evaluation with the same estimated inputs and recommendations.

## Comparators and interventions

| Arm | Implementation | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Production `RangeSolver.action_values` and DCFR. In the known-model arm the research adapter fixes both players' continuations and trains only Hero's actual root information set. | Uses up to 896 positive Villain columns by descending oracle weight with stable tie order. Run the unprojected zero-iteration value pass on every decision and use identical retained columns in the independent settlement check. |
| B | Existing `ExactRiverEV` with all physical holdings and injected combo weights and call probabilities. | Scores closing actions and wagers whose entire positive-weight response is immediate fold/call. No unsupported action is filled in. |
| C | Existing vectorized `holding_values` used as a bounded action-tree evaluator over the provided finite river menu. | Resolves every branch under the specified continuation, with float64 weighting over all compatible holdings. Score against the separate cash ledger in `action_ev_reference`. |

- **Known inputs.** All arms get the same explicit reference range and replies; A's projection is a declared computational choice. Use quarter, half, 0.8, one and 1.5 pot plus effective all-in at opening depth, half and full pot plus all-in at first raise depth, full pot plus all-in at next depth, then all-in only. The tree cap is 5,000 nodes. Hero calls at later choice points for all arms. This is the complete specified finite tree, with unchosen legal targets and later Hero continuations outside the scope of exactness. Compute the sizing gap on this same oracle tree by restricting the root to the incumbent concrete menu, keeping tail value fixed. The primary B/C choice maximizes predicted EV with lexical tie-break. A reports greedy, modal, seeded and mixed results.
- **Estimated inputs.** Trace the unmodified legacy Coach path for its exact native tree, projected reaches, full available Hero-conditioned posterior, average reply strategies and Hero continuation. Use those same inputs for B/C. Inherit nearest-ranked reply extension for holdings outside the solver's columns. Raw A estimates use its actual columns. Reprice the same tail under all four combinations of estimated/oracle posterior and replies, plus the actual projected posterior. The raw A versus projected exact gap measures numerical traversal. The projected versus full estimated gap measures reweighting at fixed replies. Swapping both sources gives the independent reference; the difference of differences measures interaction. This design does not identify how a production re-solve would respond to changed inputs. Compare A greedy and A mode/seeded against the same truth to quantify selection. Mismatched rows reuse the exact prediction and choice and change only the reference opponent.

## Outcomes and verification

- Per-action records include model and independent net bb, incumbent advantages, error, ranks, supported regret and full-menu regret. Primary aggregate endpoints are action-weighted EV MAE, concordance across pairs with reference separation greater than 0.01 bb, and mean and maximum seeded recommendation regret. Report false positive rate among advances predicted above 0.01 bb (true advance at or below 0.01 bb), individual harm below −0.01 bb relative to the sampled incumbent, and worst delta. Report modal and greedy EV and regret separately from the expectation of a mixture. Keep coverage and metrics on B's shared support alongside full-menu results. Stratify by concrete wager bucket, type of opponent and profile prefix. Count natural pure bluffs under the separate fold/call reduction using zero win/tie mass among callers. Known synthetic positive and negative controls are reported separately.
- Use paired crossed nonparametric percentile intervals (10,000 resamples, seed `55003`), independently resampling boards and training streams, keeping all styles per cell. Apply to decision-weighted MAE, seeded regret and paired seeded gain vs A. Six boards and three streams give descriptive uncertainty for this synthetic design. Raw rankings and tail risks retain their actual counts.
- Require independent payoff checks within `1e-9` chips on branch vectors and `1e-10` bb on B/C known weighted values, normalized nonnegative laws, identical Treys and production showdown results, legal concrete actions and zero unexpected fallbacks. Require independent controls for fold, check, call, size grid, ties, all-in, refund, raise/reraise, profitable and unprofitable bluffs and profitable and unprofitable short calls. Preserve all existing backend test IDs. Run focused and full backend regression, frontend production build and Coach/Match/Replay smoke. Analysis artifacts go in a fresh ignored benchmark directory.
- Holdout runs use one BLAS/OMP/MKL thread and Python hash seeds `13` and `31`. Alternate arm timing order per decision and reverse that order in the second run. Compare all deterministic row values, choices and summaries with only resource measurements excluded. Freeze a manifest of source/artifact hashes and all 72 inputs, verify it in both holdout processes, and retain incremental attempt and result logs. Leave frozen study code and schedule unchanged after the first fresh evaluation. Abort and document an invalid run if required instead of discarding rows.
- Use a 15-second per-advisor ceiling including cold replay, 768 MiB per-process high-water mark, and 1,200-second per-run deadline. Measure fresh process and warmed timing, median, nearest-rank p95, max and memory. Measure per-evaluator scaling on a separate fixed control at 1/32/128/384/896/990 positive holdings, timing eight warm repeats after the first execution. Record end-to-end Coach replay separately from scoring with an already supplied model. Known A uses 80 iterations at the root. Estimated A uses its existing independent replay rules and iteration budgets. Evaluator timings include their own ranking work if performed. Reuse the public-board matchup caches for scored holdout timings except for one cold Coach replay per process.

## Decision rule

Recommend one of retention, river replacement, hybrid or larger redesign after examining known-input parity, coverage, runtime, estimated-input regret and harm. A strong exact reference supports an independent check. Deployment as a chooser requires credible estimated-input safety evidence, engineering feasibility and later paired gameplay evidence against both incumbent and `equity`. No Phase 5A result authorizes a production change.
