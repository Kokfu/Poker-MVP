# Phase 4V — Multi-street range integrity and live pipeline validation

**Phase 4V verification complete. Architecture decision B. Repair the live replay’s opponent-range writes before integration.** The profiled Coach replay reduced 1,119 possible opponent holdings to one when it wrote back a turn CFR projection. The river then legally blocked that holding and triggered the incumbent fallback. The research adapter finished the same replay with 990 Hero-compatible holdings. It preserved the same posterior across every tested projection, and its policy differed materially from the live policy at some otherwise equivalent states. The trace itself preserved incumbent decisions and results.

## Architecture and range ownership

| Workflow | Belief lifecycle | Decision and fallback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coach | The frontend sends a manually entered prefix to the advice endpoint. The service creates a fresh advisor and uses scripted seats and a deterministic deck to replay the prefix through `HandEngine`. A named opponent’s completed local hands rebuild an `OpponentModel` snapshot and, with exploit enabled, showdown correlations. This persists profile evidence across hands. The advisor reconstructs two combo vectors from the chart and public actions on each request. New board cards mask both vectors. | `SolverBot` or `AdaptiveSolverBot` maps actions to preflop charts and postflop DCFR nodes. It reuses a street-tree descendant when the entered action matches and can re-solve from the current state after an off-tree action. It legalizes the sampled action. Coach turns on EV calculation and builds the explanation and range summaries. Solver reconstruction or range failures call `ExpertRuleBot`; invalid entered actions are rejected by the engine/service. |
| Match and Replay | The seeded runner deals through the same engine, preserves bot instances across hands, carries stacks forward, alternates buttons, and updates public profiles and showdown observations only after settlement. Solver bots reconstruct combo reach per decision and can reuse a street-root solve in the same hand. With a range-aware bot present the engine also owns sequential `PublicRangeTracker` instances, conditioned on each viewer’s cards. The Replay frontend reads the selected event from a completed history regenerated by the history service. | Only the solver family runs the Coach-style DCFR core. Match leaves the Coach EV calculation off and supplies its own history-derived profile, seed and decision identity. Engine fallback handles illegal bot action requests and records an event. A solver’s own legal Expert substitution can occur without an engine fallback event. Replay reads the recorded actions, reveals and settlements and has no independent strategy execution. |
| Research adapter | Phase 4T replays preflop chart probabilities, then maintains normalized board-legal vectors in global combo order within each reconstructed decision. It masks each board prefix and applies the public hand-dependent likelihood at each actual action state. True model zeros remain zeros. Hero cards condition the opponent report and solve compatibility. Projection copies selected weights into scratch vectors. | It solves the current postflop node once with DCFR, then legalizes the chosen action. The offline audit surfaces empty-support errors directly. The inherited production-facing bot wrapper would substitute Expert advice on a handled inference error. Phase 4U’s exact river evaluator scores legal continuations after policy construction, using isolated reference data. Flop and turn solves use equity leaves and cannot model future betting exactly. |

Coach’s built-in choices correspond to the Match API registry’s `solver` and `solver_adaptive`. The Match and Replay tabs list `solver` as their only solver choice. The Match API additionally accepts `solver_adaptive`, `solver_adaptive_gated` and `solver_adaptive_sized`. The gated and sized variants share reconstruction and DCFR but apply different lock rules. Those variants are eligible at the reconstruction and projection boundaries, with policy comparisons requiring their own matching lock implementation. The other seven registry paths are `random`, `tight`, `aggressive`, `equity`, `expert`, `adaptive`, and `range_expert`. The first four use seeded random, threshold, aggression or equity rules. Expert uses decision features, adaptive applies profile-dependent Expert adjustments, and range_expert applies a range-equity adjustment to Expert. Its engine tracker uses heuristic factors with a positive floor, renormalizes on board/action updates, and uses Monte Carlo equity before the river and exact equity on the river. It has neither CFR projection nor equivalent Phase 4T action likelihoods. ## Support and working-set integrity

The incumbent normally starts with dense reach, masks the board, then keeps columns above 0.005 of the seat’s maximum solve weight, forcing in the acting solver’s own combo. An observed action multiplies only the selected columns and initializes all other entries to zero. In the matched collapse, the selected turn column represented 65.986% of public opponent reach. The check removed only 0.00219% of that selected mass. The write erased 1,118 positive holdings through omission, before `6s` removed 100% of the surviving reach. In the unprofiled comparison the turn check still erased 147 positive holdings by projection, but 883 stayed positive after the river reveal. This distinguishes a repeated structural defect from the profile-dependent empty result. [Profiled collapse](benchmark-results/phase-4v-2026-11-19/final13/collapse-profiled.json) · [Unprofiled control](benchmark-results/phase-4v-2026-11-19/final13/collapse.json)

The research ledger conditions on actual blockers, then uses \(p(h)L(a|h)/\sum_h p(h)L(a|h)\). The denominator measures evidence before renormalization. Board deletion, likelihood-weighted reduction and scratch projection omission are recorded separately. Sparse tests remove previously positive holdings with blockers, retain genuine zeros, and prove that an impossible observation or all-supported-holdings blocker fails atomically. Research replay verifies unit normalized totals, card compatibility, a nonempty working set and legal normalized policies. ## Archived loss and uncertainty propagation

The original Phase 4U loose-passive row (board index 3, stream 213, three profile hands) reproduced an accepted adjustment of **−2.346973462 bb** under the original exact scoring law. The gate switched a 45.41% fold, 54.15% call and 0.44% shove mix to a pure shove. Exact EV moved from −0.789 bb to −3.136 bb. The original gate interval was **[+1.187, +15.332] bb**. With the original forecast reply law and fixed Hero continuation, the modeled range gave **+3.440 bb**, the reference incoming range gave **−0.380 bb**, and the reference posterior gave **−0.423 bb**. Substituting the reference replies alone on the modeled posterior still gave **+2.232 bb**. Both substitutions yielded the archived loss. Incoming range TV was 0.649 and posterior TV was 0.650. The final modeled and reference effective counts were 325.3 and 456.6. ## Phase 4W — repair the replay boundary

Use a separate controlled release proposal for the smallest repair that keeps persisted public reach independent of temporary CFR selections. Keep the current tree, chooser, engine and profile interfaces fixed while establishing the corrected inference contract in shadow execution. Audit both the observed action and its mapped solver action. A solve-column omission must have a separate ledger entry and cannot create a posterior zero. Contradictions must name the public state where valid support disappeared. Compare against the incumbent using the same geometry and expose policy changes before selecting any release arm.

Acceptance should require the profiled collapse and independent adversarial sparse and blocker cases to retain exact compatible support, no spurious fallback, explicit real contradictions, and identical posterior writes across limits and seeds at each street. Freeze a new exact-state parity set that includes unequal stacks, nonstandard blinds, multiple raises, short all-ins and reopen edges. Require legal mapped menus, conservation, untouched Match histories and policies in shadow mode, deterministic Coach and solver-Match traces, no hidden-card dependence, and the existing 15-second and 768 MiB reference checks. Carry the archived −2.347 bb case forward as evidence that a mathematically valid posterior can still be inaccurate. Any separate strategy release must clear its existing gates.
