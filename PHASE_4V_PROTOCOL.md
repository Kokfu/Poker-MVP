## Trace schema and invariants

Local JSON, schema 1. Each replay is labeled by public fixture and mode. Decision records carry hand number, ordered public history, current board, acting seat, pot, stacks, effective stack and legal menu. Range events carry street, seat, event type, action and mapped action where applicable, raw mass, positive count, effective count, normalized total and compatible support. Report attributable board deletion, observation evidence and omitted projection mass separately, with full-set and Hero-compatible projection statistics where applicable. Solver records carry root geometry, selected column counts, cached status, legalized mix, choice, action EV where available, and fallback reason. Settlement records carry public event geometry and chip transfers. Timing and memory are separate. Exclude private cards, per-combo weights or identities, complete hidden reference ranges, undealt runouts, and deck state from output.

Require exact traced versus untraced actions, full-precision solver frequencies and deterministic explanations or public histories. Compare all deterministic panel records across the two hash seeds. Check each reconstructed decision against engine pot, stacks, commitments, actor and street. Report reopening, last raise and menu disagreements if found. Require legal normalized policies, chip conservation through every event, correct refunds, exact blockers, normalization within 1e-12 and no resurrected support. For research, compare full arrays in process for persistent weights and observed-action likelihoods across budgets and seeds, and compare the selected mapping to actual CFR input lengths. Positive support can decrease from blockers or genuine zero likelihood, independent of computational omission. Any incumbent support loss attributable to projection fails integration readiness, even if its chosen action is unchanged. Expected negative controls are the archived incumbent collapse and explicit contradiction. Record any other fallback and inspect the triggering history. Missing outputs or timeouts fail coverage.
