# Phase 4T protocol

## Coupling audit recorded before implementation

Coach validates public actions and reveals board prefixes through the hand engine. The incumbent reconstructs public reach for both seats from the chart, then calls a street-root CFR solve. Its cutoff keeps hands above 0.005 times the largest legal reach. The observed action is mapped onto that tree and the solver average is read at its node. The update writes zero for every omitted combination even if the hand was possible. The next observation, blocker update and final Hero-compatible normalization all inherit the deletion. In Phase 4S the omitted tail receives a pooled likelihood computed from the retained reach. Both that average and the individual evaluated values can change when columns change, because the projected game itself changes. Neither method uses an explicit random sample of holdings. Selection by reach and the incumbent sampled flop runouts can bias modeled probabilities through different mechanisms. In particular, reach-based pooling treats a selected subset as representative of unselected hands. Equilibrium and locked strategies may put almost no mass on an observed action, narrowing posteriors that can be inaccurate near a closing price threshold.

Current and earlier street solves dominate replay time in Phase 4S. Its measured explicit reach updates and projection were small. Its earlier solve costs function as inference costs because replay needs the corresponding policy rows. The live decision strategy and continuation values also use selected columns. Future responses to Hero branches affect nonclosing EV and need a separate diagnostic. They cannot affect the value of a terminal fold/cover choice.

Unavailable aggression is reassigned to calls. Classify effective closing targets and map other off-menu sizes to the nearest eligible log-size bucket. Use the same uncalibrated observation law for the Hero public reach used by CFR. No actual Villain hand or later board is passed to this law.

Reuse existing confidence-weighted public street frequency targets for Villain where available. Fit the prior law to those aggregate targets over **full board-legal persistent reach** using the existing proportional fitting routine and shrink by its confidence weight. These frequency targets do not identify hand-specific bluff and value proportions. Cache board-only tier vectors with a bounded LRU. A board reveal or observation never adds support. Conditional on Hero cards, use the resulting legal normalized posterior for reporting and sensitivity. Zero evidence or empty compatible support raises an explicit replay error.

Feed future reply forecasts only from the final solve to the solver EV path. The uncertainty arm uses the full conditional posterior and the Phase 4S [1,2] odds rule unchanged.
