# Phase 4N — Fixed protocol for bet-conditioned river ranges

The experiment estimates mean paired exact EV per facing-bet decision (bb), equally weighted within the matched holdout, with production replies frozen at their baseline per-holding probabilities. A second setting uses true fold/call replies to isolate range error. Both settings score all policies against the same true posterior and true replies. Per row, keep the concrete menu, original hero continuation after a modeled re-raise, and 80-step fixed-value decision rule. Use the effective stack and actual pot for every payoff. Preserve live advice as an audit; the controlled baseline substitutes only the common root selection rule.

Candidate: take the production prior on the first river solve's support, including preceding street actions. Remove blocked combinations. Sum the existing aggression likelihoods for the pooled component. Interpolate neighboring abstract likelihoods in log total-target space, clamping below the first anchor and honoring the exact shove anchor. Normalize the pooled and local components under the same prior so the mixture's confidence does not depend on the abstract actions' marginal frequencies. Use weights 5% incoming prior, and the remaining 95% split into overall betting evidence and interpolated size evidence. Give size evidence share `n/(n+20)` where `n` is the previous completed profile's river opening bet count. A zero-mass local estimate backs off to pooled betting; a zero-mass pooled estimate returns the incoming prior. No parameters are fitted and no oracle parameters or cards enter the candidate. The effective prior cannot revive combos dropped by the first solve. Compare that prior and a second-prune ablation with the independently known ranges.

Reference: reuse Phase 4K preflop participation, raise, opening-frequency and response laws for the four synthetic types. Specify conditional size allocation on .50P, .80P, 1.35P and shove; high card gets .20/.40/.27/.13, one pair .60/.28/.10/.02 and two pair or better .22/.30/.27/.21. Cap by stack, floor by minimum, add collided size mass. These choices make actual size informative without fitting to a historical loss or using the candidate's size calculation. On the button Hero raises and Villain calls. In the big blind Hero raises after Villain limps; both Villain calls have the independently specified participation/no-raise requirement. Flop and turn check through. When Hero is in the big blind Hero checks before the observed river bet. Payoffs use equal starting stacks, 50/100 blinds, varying preflop targets and 9/15/30 bb stacks. The reference facing a raise can fold or call only. Compare full posterior with both independent enumerations. Group holdings into high card / one pair / two pair or better as composition proxies; made-hand strength does not identify actual betting intent.

Profile training uses real public histories from the established Phase 4K reference bots, independent of both evaluation panels. Their legacy opening sizing is fixed/capped at 500, so histories match each reference's opening action propensity but not its new conditional size law. The candidate uses opening bet counts and existing locked policies; this test does not learn hand-by-size frequencies from directly labeled bets. For each opponent and stream, use 3, 30 and 100 completed history prefixes. The calibration deal seed is 434901 (four deals, training stream 61). The holdout deal seed is 434902 (12 new deals, streams 71/72, both positions per deal). The holdout has 288 matched decisions and 288 separate mismatch comparisons using the opposite profile on both style axes. Repetitions within a board are diagnostic, not independent deals. No holdout deals or fitted profile data are used in development. Use 10,000 paired board-block resamples (seed 3349), preserving all profiles, positions, sizes and depths assigned to each board, conditional on the two sampled profile streams. These intervals do not estimate sampling uncertainty over the population of profiles.

Promotion requires all of the following:

- Production-response mean paired gain at least +0.05 bb and lower paired 95% interval above zero. True-response mean gain at least +0.02 bb.
- No matched opponent/type mean below −0.03 bb in either setting; production-response mismatch mean at least −0.05 bb.
- In both settings, worst harm at most 0.50 bb and no more than 10% of matched decisions lose more than 0.01 bb. Also report count and sum of losses exceeding 1e-6 bb and retained regret.
- Matched posterior TV improves by at least 0.01; multiclass excess Brier and absolute equity error do not regress. No illegal combos, invalid mass, illegal recommendations or unexpected fallbacks. All reference payoffs must cross-check with Phase 4J.
- Median incremental range update below 5 ms and worst traced incremental allocation peak below 256 KiB. Timing includes validating the already extracted prior and policy and constructing the new distribution. Report process peak and live times separately; do not treat process peak as incremental overhead.

An unmet condition rejects promotion. Do not retune on the held-out set. If rejected, the next recommendation must distinguish residual prior error, posterior likelihood error, confidence and decision/response effects, or retain production if evidence is insufficient.
