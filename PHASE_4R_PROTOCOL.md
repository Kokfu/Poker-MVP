# Phase 4R frozen support experiment

The candidate is the research-only `SupportReplay` advisor. Set the postflop relative solve cutoff to zero. Give every positive board-legal holding a column, and use the same column likelihood for the next reach update. Preserve model zeros, chart selection, betting trees, lock rules, DCFR iterations and the seeded chooser. Validate raw reach and the existence of an actual Hero-compatible posterior. On genuinely empty support the advisor uses the existing legal fallback with a typed research reason. Every such fallback still fails the reliability gate. The phase makes no production changes.

Both advisors receive identical Coach spots and frozen prefixes of independently generated public hand histories. No candidate parameters are fitted. Use the Phase 4Q case generator and reference hand policies. Development uses deal seed 438201, four boards and stream 141. The independent holdout uses deal seed 438202, eight boards crossed with four fixed styles and three training streams 151–153, for 96 rows. Prefixes 0, 3, 30 and 100 rotate by board and stream across 9, 15 and 30 bb starting stacks. The schedule includes different positions, opening sizes and earlier action lines. The Phase 4Q failure and two extreme archive rows are diagnostic fixtures only. Their original reference laws remain fixed.

Compare each advisor's actual full-precision mixed strategy. The reference sums exact river payoffs over the same independent legal opponent posterior and call policy on the same concrete Hero menu. Audit payoff and normalization against independent Bayesian and tree calculations. The primary metric is the equal-weight paired mean candidate minus baseline EV in bb per decision. Report regret to the best supported action, modal changes, action shares, posterior support and effective size. A failed pair stays in the reliability denominator with its reason and observed fallback action. Complete cases may be described, and a failed pair precludes a full-panel promotion estimate.

Preserve the Phase 4Q zero-unexpected-fallback gate for both live advisor paths. Require zero illegal actions, invalid probabilities and conservation failures. The minimum primary gain is +0.05 bb with a 95% paired interval strictly above zero. Each opponent mean must be at least −0.03 bb. The maximum individual loss is 0.50 bb, with at most 10% of rows losing more than 0.01 bb. Use a 10,000-draw crossed percentile bootstrap with RNG seed 43803, resampling board indices and whole profile streams independently and holding all four styles together in each cell. Three streams and four fixed styles limit generalization. The phase requires reproducibility across Python hash seeds 13 and 31 with one BLAS thread, ignoring runtime and peak memory. Candidate replay median must be at most 2× the incumbent median, maximum 15 seconds per decision, and the combined runner peak at most 768 MiB. Bound each holdout process externally at 1,200 seconds.

For diagnosis, vary the mixture of the archived bad call posterior and the wager-oracle posterior at 0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100%. Keep the respective incoming prior fixed. Report posterior TV moved, cover EV and exact greedy regret. Across the fresh closing-wager panel, count modeled margins within one and five equity points. Since showdown outcome per holding is in [0, 1], equity can move by at most the TV perturbation, and closing cover EV by at most the final pot times TV. Apply no decision rule based on these margins in this experiment.

Freeze the candidate, protocol, schedule, training, truth, and shared solver sources by SHA-256 before opening the independent panel. Holdout success would make a separately preregistered gameplay comparison eligible. The output is conditional exact river EV under scripted reference policies.
