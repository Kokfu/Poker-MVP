# Phase 4S protocol

## Ownership and invariants recorded before implementation

Coach and the hand engine own public observation, geometry and street order. The chart and replay own both seats' public reach. Board cards permanently exclude impossible holdings at the street boundary. The actual Hero cards are used to form conditional estimates and to ensure a usable decision column. They do not delete counterfactual public range columns.

Positive public reach is carried across street transitions in global combo-index order. A bounded projection supplies the CFR tree and retains its own column mapping, weights and mass fractions. Applying an action uses its solved likelihood on evaluated combinations. The omitted holdings receive the pooled reach-weighted likelihood of that action among evaluated holdings. This keeps their prior odds relative to one another and treats the unevaluated holdings as a single exchangeable group. It is an inference approximation with no claim of real-world calibration. An exact zero on an evaluated holding can eliminate it. If the pooled likelihood is zero while omitted positive reach exists, report unsupported inference and use the existing legal fallback. A genuinely empty public or Hero-compatible range also uses that fallback with a specific reason. No impossible holdings or zero preaction weights can gain mass.

Keep the incumbent relative cutoff and the forced actual Hero column. Bound each working set to 896 columns, with weights ranked by descending reach and ties assigned by global combo ID. Reserve the highest-weight compatible Villain column if the ordinary selection contains none. Preserve all trees, locks, iteration counts, card equity and seeded action selection. The cap and the inference projection are both part of the architecture factor. Advice can change where any of these alters the inputs. Exact policy parity is expected when the same columns and action likelihoods are used and omitted positive support is absent.

## Decision factor

Treat a closing fold/call on the river as a terminal choice. For nominal mass p on a compatible holding, let alternative mass be proportional to p times a likelihood multiplier in [1, 2]. This represents an odds distortion of up to 2 between any two supported holdings. The factor of 2 is an interpretable sensitivity scale, chosen before evaluation and held fixed across profile sizes. It has no frequentist confidence interpretation. The envelope cannot add support to a misspecified posterior. Measure its empirical coverage against independent reference equity and report coverage by prefix. Evaluate the lower and upper EV over this set using the three showdown outcomes. Restrict the incumbent mixed strategy to fold when a call is nonpositive at the lower bound. Otherwise retain its full mix. This conservative rule leaves nonclosing choices and the original sample unchanged where the bound clears the price. No reference opponent identity, reference probabilities or unrevealed cards enter the rule. On fallback the associated uncertainty arm has exactly the parent arm's fallback.

A is incumbent. B is architecture only. C applies the decision rule to A's posterior. D applies the decision rule to B's persistent posterior. B's CFR and explanation EV still use its working set. C and D share the identically computed parent replay and recommendations use the same seeded chooser. Score fallbacks as actual one-action policies where the exact menu is available, keep them in the reliability denominator, and fail the promotion gate for unexpected fallbacks.

Use the existing Phase 4Q case generator and reference, with fresh deal seed 439201 and profile stream 161 for development. Each stream supplies independent profile training hands, with prefixes 0, 3, 30 and 100 rotated across the same 9, 15 and 30 bb schedule as Phase 4R. Historical diagnostics retain the Phase 4Q reference laws and are excluded from promotion. No fitting or tuning uses development or holdout EV.

Primary contrast D−A. Report B−A, C−A, D−B and the paired interaction (D−B)−(C−A). Use 10,000 crossed percentile bootstrap replicates with seed 43903, resampling boards and streams independently and keeping styles together in each cell. Styles are fixed, with only three independent profile streams. Secondary intervals are descriptive. Any separately considered candidate must meet the same gates on its own A contrast. Report all harms over 0.01 bb and identify losses exceeding 0.50 bb.

Require mean gain of at least 0.05 bb per decision, 95% lower bound strictly positive, no paired loss larger than 0.50 bb, at most 10% harmful adjustments over 0.01 bb, and mean above or equal to −0.03 bb for each style. Require legal normalized advice, exact payoff checks, chip conservation and zero unexpected fallbacks for all arms. Keep the 15-second maximum, candidate median at most 2× incumbent median, and 768 MiB process resident peak. Measure full traced replay and incremental decision-rule runtime. Warm each board's equity matrices before the paired comparisons and also measure separate cold replay in profiling. Alternate parent order deterministically. Run processes with one BLAS thread and a 1,200-second wall bound per holdout process, repeat with hash seeds 13 and 31, and compare the deterministic results ignoring performance fields. Freeze source, executable inputs, candidate constants, this protocol and the generated holdout cases before any holdout replay. No changes based on holdout results. A pass would justify only a separately preregistered duplicate-deal, seat-swapped gameplay experiment.
